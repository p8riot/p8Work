    Import details & cleanup rules
    • A newer .docx for the same named month replaces that schedule. Files are parsed in this browser and are not uploaded to a server.
    • Shift codes are configurable in Setup. This workspace starts with D → 7a–3p, E → 3p–11p, and N → 11p–7a.
    • Imported staff display as first name + last initial by default. Roster aliases can correct spelling variations and repeated boss typos.
    • Case, spacing, missing meridiems such as 1-9p, and obvious daytime typos such as 9P-5P are normalized.
    • A blank staff cell inside a schedule is treated as Off, while the original blank is retained.
    • The month/year in the filename anchors dates even when the schedule starts in the previous month or ends in the next month.
    • The original cell text is retained in saved data alongside the cleaned value.
    • Imported working shifts use your configurable default location. Locations can be added, renamed, deactivated, or removed when unused.
    • Manual shift edits are saved as overrides, so replacing an imported monthly schedule does not silently erase your adjustments.

Chores & Assignment Rules

Leave eligible rooms blank for a whole-house rotation. Room-restricted chores only Auto-Roll among active residents in those rooms. Manual assignments may override the room rule. Difficulty is 1 (Easy), 2 (Moderate), or 3 (Hard). Auto-Roll uses a Fairness Guard that gives priority to residents with fewer Hard assignments, then fewer combined Moderate and Hard assignments, before total workload and history are used as tie-breakers.
